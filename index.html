Coperni

Air quality forecasts on a map

PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃ and SO₂ for the next 48 hours around any Italian municipality or European city, from the Copernicus (CAMS) forecasts, compared with EU thresholds and the European Air Quality Index. Includes a daily ranking of cities.

Open the map · City ranking · Source code

p7m-apri

Open .p7m files and Italian e-invoices

Extract the document from a digitally signed .p7m file and check the signature, without installing anything. Turns FatturaPA e-invoices (XML or .p7m) into a readable PDF. Files are deleted after one hour.

Open a .p7m file · E-invoice viewer · Source code

Attese

Health waiting times in South Tyrol

Waiting times for medical visits and tests in Alto Adige/Südtirol, from the provincial open data: compare hospitals and districts and see how times change over time. In Italian.

See waiting times

fatturapa

Python library

Zero-dependency parser for Italian electronic invoices (FatturaPA 1.2, FPR12/FPA12) into Python dataclasses. It powers the p7m-apri invoice viewer.
